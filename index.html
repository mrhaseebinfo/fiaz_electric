FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Still confused about something? Call or WhatsApp us anytime.

Do you provide warranty on electric products? ⌄

Yes. Every product we sell — lights, fans, irons, heaters, cameras and appliances — comes with an official brand warranty card and a proper receipt. LED lights carry 1–2 years warranty depending on the brand.

Do you offer home visit service for electrical repairs? ⌄

Absolutely. Our certified electricians visit your home for wiring faults, breaker trips, fan or light installation and repairs. Same-day service is available in most areas — call or WhatsApp us to book a slot.

Can you install a complete solar system at my house? ⌄

Yes, we install complete on-grid, off-grid and hybrid solar systems. We start with a free roof survey, then provide a written quotation with panel, inverter and battery options. Most homes save 50–70% on their electricity bill.

Do you handle commercial and shop wiring projects? ⌄

Yes. We wire offices, shops, factories and marriage halls with three-phase systems, distribution boards, emergency lighting and CCTV — all up to safety standards with testable wiring.

Which home automation products do you offer? ⌄

We supply and install smart switches, WiFi breakers, smart locks, motion sensors, app-controlled lights and smart CCTV systems — all controllable from your phone, even when you are away from home.

What are your shop timings and payment methods? ⌄

We are open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM (short Friday prayer break). We accept cash, bank transfer and easy paisa wallets on all orders.
